ABOUT THIS EVENT

Where technology meets opportunity

Nairobi Tech Meetup brings together developers, students, tech enthusiasts, entrepreneurs and innovators for an exciting day of learning and networking.

Discover new ideas, share your experiences, explore emerging technologies and meet people who are passionate about building solutions through technology. Whether you are starting your tech journey or already have experience, there is something for you.
